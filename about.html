The Life Of Nelson Mandela

About Nelson Mandela

Nelson Mandela was a South African leader who dedicated his life to fighting apartheid and promoting equality, freedom, and peace.

Quick Facts

  • Born: July 18, 1918
  • Birthplace: Mvezo, South Africa
  • Country: South Africa
  • Known for: Fighting apartheid and promoting equality
  • Became president: 1994

His Early Journey

Mandela studied law and became involved in the struggle against apartheid, a system that enforced racial separation and inequality in South Africa. His dedication to justice eventually made him one of the country's most important leaders.

A Life Of Change

1918

Nelson Mandela was born in Mvezo, South Africa.

1944

He became involved in the African National Congress Youth League.

1990

Mandela was released from prison after 27 years.

1994

He became South Africa's first Black president.

Mandela's life remains a powerful example of courage, perseverance, forgiveness, and the pursuit of equality. His story continues to inspire people around the world.
